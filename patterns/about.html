Redefining Modern Essentials

Founded in 2020, Modern Core began with a simple belief: everyday essentials should be extraordinary. We set out to create clothing that combines timeless design with sustainable practices — pieces that feel as good as they look and last for years, not seasons.

Every garment in our collection is crafted from responsibly sourced materials in factories that share our commitment to ethical production. We don't follow trends. We focus on perfecting the basics.
